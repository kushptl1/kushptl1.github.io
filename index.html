01About

Day to day I work in identity and access management: reviewing entitlements, remediating compromised credentials, and automating the joiner-mover-leaver workflows that decide who can reach what. Healthcare makes that unforgiving. Access has to be tight enough to survive an audit and fast enough that nobody waits on a login.

I'm extending that into cloud security, building AWS environments with Terraform and CloudFormation so the same least-privilege thinking applies to infrastructure.

Day job

Hospital access I can defend in a review

Most of my IAM work comes down to two questions. Can clinicians reach what they need, and can I prove they can't reach anything else? In practice that means validating entitlements, remediating compromised credentials, and handling access when people join, change roles, or leave.

Controls

Least privilege, access reviews, lifecycle checkpoints, and remediation that leaves an audit trail.

Tooling

Active Directory, Azure AD, BeyondTrust, CrowdStrike Falcon Identity Protection, and ServiceNow.
